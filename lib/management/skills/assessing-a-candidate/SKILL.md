# Assessing a candidate

First prove independence: the examiner must not appear as this candidate's
instructor or mentor. Resolve the baseline and enumerate its complete
competency denominator. For each competency, inspect resolvable demonstration
witnesses and record `demonstrated` or `not-demonstrated`; never infer success
from attempt counts, confidence, reputation, shared model family, or trainer
approval. Append an `Assessment` with limitations. If any competency is not
demonstrated, disposition is `gaps`.
