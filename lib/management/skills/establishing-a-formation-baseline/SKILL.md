# Establishing a formation baseline

Read the exact admitted office, duties, skills, jurisdiction, protected-context
policy, competencies, and assessment law. Recover Academy-owned doctrine and
customer overlays by stable identity and digest. Build constructive alignment:
each competency must appear in formation activities and an independent
demonstration. Include orientation, simulation, shadow work, supervised
practice, independent demonstration, reflection, and teaching-back where they
are meaningful; do not turn the stages into seat-time requirements.

Append a `CurriculumRevision` with doctrine sources, omissions, and parent
revisions. Then append a `FormationBaseline` that copies the admitted office
snapshot exactly. Never edit a predecessor or assert a global latest revision.
