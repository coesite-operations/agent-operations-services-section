# Recording succession practice

Name whether the exercise concerns incumbent handover, office succession, or a
curriculum bridge. Resolve the candidate enrollment and baseline. Preserve the
open-obligation inventory, contradictions, protected-context transfer mode, and
witnesses. Append an Academy-local exercise whose `customerMutation` and
`performsReceiverAct` are false. The exercise can qualify succession practice;
it cannot relieve, decommission, invest, or appoint for the receiver.
