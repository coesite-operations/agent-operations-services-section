# Recording candidate formation

Resolve the enrollment, baseline, candidate layer bindings, and bounded grant.
Record the stage, instructor, mentor where applicable, exact competencies,
evidence digests, outcome, and corrections. Preserve failures and incomplete
work; training evidence is not a promotion counter. The Academy records its
observation only and never writes the practicum customer's record.
