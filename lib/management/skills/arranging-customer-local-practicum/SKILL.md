# arranging customer local practicum

Contract for shadow work, supervised practice, or independent demonstration at the customer's boundary. Name the customer officer who controls real work, the Academy observation grant, protected context, feedback, interruption, and egress; ensure every operational write remains customer-local.

The completion condition is one field placement whose practice is real and whose authority remains customer-owned. Record omissions and contradictory evidence; do not round absence to success.
