# Arranging customer-local practicum

This is the Academy's **existing placement procedure**, not another trainer, Academy credential, source of customer Work, or permission to operate. The receiving settlement owns the office, original Work, operational admission, protection and acceptance. The Academy owns formation evidence. The independent examiner and registrar remain separate.

## 1. Admit a situated placement contract

Resolve the original **FormationRequest**, **Enrollment**, exact office epoch and deontic profile, **FormationBaseline**, source-identified competencies, candidate assembly and customer Work identity. Name the receiver officer who controls the Work, the Academy practitioner, any mentor, the separately eligible examiner, and the source-owned operational procedure. Distinguish a standing Duty, bounded Task, Work, Session, Game Turn and Move; a placement creates none of these by renaming it.

Record the practice mode (shadow, simulated, supervised or independent demonstration); permitted customer operations and effects; observation grant; identity of the tool provider and intended beneficiary; expected outcome and negative control; protected-context handling, allowed egress and retention; resource/time budget, interrupt and escalation limits; and who may independently receive. The customer must grant each real mutation at its own boundary. An Academy TrainingGrant with `customerMutation:false` authorizes **observation and Academy-local evidence only**, not customer writes. Customer rights never pass through the Academy, a model, an Agent Card or a skill manifest.

Separate **declared**, **available**, **authorized**, **invoked**, **effectful**, and **received** capability. Require the actual executor's current session and admitted operation contract for live practice; a catalog declaration or a successful read-only call is not an effect. If an operator, session, provider, right, source or budget is missing, preserve the original Work and record its specific obstruction using the incumbent FormationRefusal / Work mechanism. Do not simulate a completed live operation, invent an office or start a substitute scheduler.

## 2. Exercise with progressively real responsibility

Begin with a source-pinned simulation or shadow pass, then obtain the customer officer's explicit permission for any bounded supervised real duty. Use the *original* source provider and Work identifier throughout, including on retry. The practitioner must demonstrate one lawful positive case with independently inspectable output **and** appropriate adverse cases for the real duty, such as:

- a well-formed message or content hash claiming a stage completion that earlier admitted state does not authorize; refuse the false result rather than accepting the green surface;
- a tool discovered but unavailable, a request with insufficient authority, or a missing live Session; keep the source Work obstructed rather than calling an attempted invocation successful;
- interruption, replay or duplicate arrival; compare canonical event head and prior effects before retry, resume the original Work, and never double-apply a non-idempotent move;
- a handoff between distinct legitimate operator sessions with unresolved duties, protected context and an independent receiver; do not equate a second prompt, mask or shared model call with a separate examiner.

The source of each case is the actual relevant Office/Duty/Work and provider interface, not this generic list. A simulation must be labeled simulated; its apparent success cannot certify live authority or deployment. Record a refusal as a valid judgment when the authorized outcome is to decline. Accessibility and language of demonstration derive from the actual candidate, receiver, content and encounter, not a universal default modality.

## 3. Witness and recover

For an admitted enrollment, use the **original Academy TrainingEvent** carrier, not a new practice ledger. Bind it to the exact enrollment, baseline, bundle, candidate assembly, instructor, practitioner character/runtime/agent-relation digests, stage, competency identifiers and event time. Each evidence item must preserve a verifiable digest of the embedded artifact, the exact case IDs, witness kind, linked source Work/provider response, and the difference between an operation attempt, origin processing and downstream beneficiary receipt. `TrainingEvent.customerMutation` remains **false**. Preserve opposed evidence and unknown costs rather than reporting success by omission.

If the provider or session fails, stop under the customer's incident boundary, preserve original Work and prior receipts, and record an explicit failure/refusal and recovery owner; do not silently replay an irreversible side effect. For a changed source, authority or baseline, use the Academy's existing successor configuration and requalification-gap methods, never overwrite an issued qualification or past training event.

## 4. Independent receiving

Deliver the source-identifiable placement and TrainingEvent evidence to the existing `assessing-a-candidate` procedure. The examiner must be independently qualified, distinct from the candidate's instructors/mentors and permitted to inspect the **complete** baseline competency denominator. For each case, independently resolve actual provider effects and the receiving officer's judgment, including adverse controls and missing evidence. The examiner—not the Academy practitioner or candidate—decides whether an Assessment is `qualified` or `gaps`. An assessment does **not** grant execution authority, seat an officer, or discharge customer Work; only the receiving settlement can admit its own outcomes and incumbency.

**Completion condition:** one customer-authorized, bounded field placement with genuine practice or an explicit evidenced refusal, Academy-local TrainingEvent/FormationRefusal evidence, and an independently receivable case-by-case handoff. A favorable training result without a real authorized duty effect and beneficiary receipt must not be labeled operational delivery.
