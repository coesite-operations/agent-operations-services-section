# Compiling an office formation bundle

1. Resolve the enrollment, cohort, formation baseline, customer office snapshot, curriculum, and assessment law.
2. Census the relevant doctrine before compression. Separate current law, current practice, admitted research, legacy prior art, and customer overlays. State the denominator and every omission.
3. Keep office, character, situated persona, candidate substrate, skills, tools/capabilities, context carriers, runtime, and incumbency distinct.
4. Assemble complete skill bodies and purpose-specific carriers. Reuse doctrine suits, query suits, capsules, orientation surfaces, gap bundles, and existing harness projectors through explicit provider contracts; do not invent a universal packet or silently import another settlement's runtime.
5. Attach orientation, rehearsal, supervised practice, refusal and negative-control cases, succession practice, reflection, and teaching-back appropriate to the exact office.
6. Name practitioner-lecturer, mentor, simulation, research, and projector roles as engagement roles or providers. Do not add them to the Academy roster merely because one formation uses them.
7. Bind the result to the cohort's content-addressed formation configuration and candidate identity. Record omissions and withheld authority.
8. Return the bundle to the customer or candidate. The customer alone recognizes, inducts, installs, and grants capabilities.
