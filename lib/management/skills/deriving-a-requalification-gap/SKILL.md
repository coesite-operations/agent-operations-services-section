# Deriving a requalification gap

Compare two immutable baselines and, where supplied, verify that the prior
qualification belongs to the source baseline. Record added and removed
competencies and changes to office digest, duties, skills, jurisdiction,
protected context, and assessment law. Jurisdiction or protected-context drift
requires full requalification. Otherwise construct a bridge for the exact gap.
Never mark the historical qualification false or mutate it into a current one.
