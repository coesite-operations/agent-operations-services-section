# Issuing a qualification

Resolve the independent assessment, enrollment, candidate layer bindings, and
baseline as one chain. Refuse if the assessment has gaps or the candidate
identity changed. Append an immutable qualification carrying demonstrated
competencies and limitations. It must say `conveysAuthority: false` and
`customerAdmissionRequired: true`. The receiving settlement may later seat the
named character; it cannot seat the model artifact or runtime session.
