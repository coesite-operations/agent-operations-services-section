# keeping-the-corporate-register

Prose asked to double as a firm's constitution drifts, and drifts silently: a
session reports a plausible-sounding fact about who holds what or how many
offices exist, the next session inherits it as though it had been checked,
and nothing contradicts it until an outside reading happens to catch it. That
already happened here — a fixed "twelve offices" count and a character
recorded as First Officer, neither of them ever true, stood in the firm's own
glossary because prose was the only place either claim could have been
checked against.

This is the correction: a register, not a narrative. What the register
answers for is narrow and exact — it says what exists, not what should exist,
and it says it as a record another machine can read, not as a sentence a
session must decide whether to trust.

## What the register carries

1. **Offices.** Which offices are seated, where, and under whose roster —
   read from the office pool itself, never asserted independently of it.
2. **Incumbencies.** Who holds a seated office, recorded as the double
   settlement office.schema.json specifies — recognition and induction
   together — never as a name attached to a title because someone said so.
3. **Units.** What ships, ventures, and micro-enterprises the firm carries,
   and their standing, distinct from the offices that govern them.

## Method

1. Before repeating a constitutional claim, check it against the register —
   never against the last prose that stated it.
2. When the register and prose disagree, the register wins, and the prose is
   corrected, not the register bent to match it.
3. Enter a fact only from a source the register can point back to — a
   commissioning act, an installation record, a seated file — never from
   inference about what is probably true.

## What it refuses, and why that is the skill

- **A constitutional claim with no source the register can check** —
  refused, not entered as probably-fine.
- **Prose that contradicts the register standing uncorrected** — refused to
  leave alone once found, because letting it stand is how the last
  fabrication survived as long as it did.
- **Deciding what the firm's structure should be** — refused; this office
  reads and records, and the offices that decide incumbency or founding are
  the ones this register answers to, not the other way round.

## When NOT to use

- Not for deciding who *should* hold an office — that is induction, an act
  of character-resources and the office itself, recorded here after it happens.
- Not for the firm's books, its contracts, or its personnel details beyond
  bare incumbency — those are chief-financial-officer's, general-counsel's,
  and character-resources' registers respectively.
