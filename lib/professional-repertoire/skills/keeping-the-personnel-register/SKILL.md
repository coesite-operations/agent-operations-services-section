# keeping-the-personnel-register

A person was recorded as First Officer without ever having held the role,
and nothing caught it, because incumbency lived in prose that nobody was
checking against a register — because there was no register. Office.schema.json
already states the theory this office has to make operational: incumbency is
the double settlement of recognition (backward, history-sensitive) and
induction (forward-committed); vacant is (bottom, bottom), dignitas is (t,t),
and a top in either cell is a repair condition, not a fact to paper over.

The same register is where the three rights get recorded honestly. A firm
telling a unit it holds decision, personnel, and distribution rights while
recording only two of them has, by sanquan's own standard, given the unit a
department dressed as an enterprise — and the only way to catch that gap is
to record what a unit actually holds, not what it was told.

## What the register carries

1. **Incumbency, per office.** Recognition and induction as separate cells,
   not collapsed into a name; vacant recorded as vacant, never as an empty
   list that reads as an oversight.
2. **The three rights, per unit.** Decision, personnel, distribution —
   recorded individually, so a unit missing one of them is visible as
   missing it rather than assumed to have all three because it is called an
   enterprise.
3. **Mutual selection events.** A unit's members choosing a leader, and any
   subsequent removal — recorded in both directions, since guanbing-huxuan's
   whole content is that selection runs both ways.

## Method

1. Before repeating who holds an office, check the register; a claim the
   register cannot source is not repeated as fact.
2. Record induction and recognition as they happen, not retroactively
   reconstructed from whichever session remembers it.
3. When a unit's rights are partial, record the gap — do not round a unit
   holding two of three rights up to "has the three rights."

## What it refuses, and why that is the skill

- **An incumbency claim the register cannot source** — refused, not entered
  on the strength of someone having said so.
- **The three rights recorded as a set when only some are held** — refused;
  partial delegation is recorded as partial, because rounding it up produces
  a department every time regardless of what the unit is called.
- **Deciding who should hold an office** — refused; this office records the
  settlement, and does not substitute its own judgment for the recognition
  and induction that make the settlement real.

## When NOT to use

- Not for appraising a unit's project work — that is a project lead's act
  under IPD, distinct from this office's staffing and capability-building
  duty.
- Not for the corporate register of offices and units themselves, or for the
  contract a unit signs — those are corporate-secretary's and
  general-counsel's.
