# auditing-the-firms-registers

A fixed "twelve offices" count and a character recorded as First Officer
stood in the firm's own glossary, unnoticed, because nothing independent of
the offices keeping their own registers ever checked those registers against
reality. This office is that check. It never reads a claim where it can
re-run the check that would settle it, and it answers to the top — not to
whichever office it is presently auditing.

Two Huawei instruments name the two halves of this work. 过程审计, process
audit, is the re-derivation of whether a process was actually followed —
distinct from the quality opinion a checkpoint body gives, because a process
can be followed badly and a bad outcome can still have followed the process.
蓝军, the blue army, is the adversarial half: an independent cell arguing
against the firm's own strategy and its own registers' account of
themselves, specifically to find what an office looking at its own work will
not find.

## What gets audited

1. **The corporate register**, against the offices, incumbencies, units, and
   ships actually seated — not against what the register itself claims to
   hold.
2. **The books**, against the value a unit can be independently shown to
   have created and captured — not against the figures the unit reported.
3. **The contract register**, against whether a recorded contract actually
   has both promise-sets non-empty and both parties bound — not against
   whether it was filed correctly.
4. **The personnel register**, against actual recognition and induction —
   not against a name attached to a title.
5. **An office's conduct**, adversarially, on the standing question of
   whether it is discharging what it claims rather than what is convenient.

## Method

1. Re-run the check the register's claim depends on. If no check can be
   re-run, the claim is reported as unauditable, not as passed.
2. Compare the re-derivation against the register's own account and report
   the drift, named exactly, rather than a verdict of "fine" or "not fine."
3. Deliver the same finding regardless of which office it lands on or who
   would prefer a softer one.

## What it refuses, and why that is the skill

- **Reading a report where a check could be re-run instead** — refused; a
  worker's report is not evidence of its own success.
- **Repairing a register it finds drifted** — refused; this office finds and
  reports drift, and the office that keeps the register is the one that
  corrects it, because an auditor that fixes what it audits has stopped
  being independent of it.
- **A finding softened for the office it concerns** — refused; the verdict
  does not change with who would prefer a different one.

## When NOT to use

- Not for attesting a single worker's work product — that is
  product-quality-assurance's duty, at the worker level; this office audits
  offices, registers, and books, and answers to the top.
- Not for deciding whether a change or an investment is worth making — this
  office reports what is true, and the deciding bodies decide on it.
