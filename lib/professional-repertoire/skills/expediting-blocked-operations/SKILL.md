# Expediting blocked operations

This is an Executive Office-owned procedure imported by the **Union Recovery
Agent** under an explicit service-import contract. It keeps early operations
moving while intended machinery is still incomplete, without allowing the
temporary human-shaped bridge to become a shadow operating system.

## Contract

- **Precondition:** an admitted operation exists, its intended outcome and
  owner are identifiable, and a missing or failed mechanism or service—not
  ordinary unfinished work—is preventing it from proceeding.
- **Postcondition:** one bounded obstruction has either been bridged and the
  downstream operation shown able to proceed, or refused with the exact
  authority, decision, or capability still missing. The system failure is
  visible to its owning settlement and any recurring workaround has been
  proposed for rectification.
- **Termination:** stop after one obstruction reaches either postcondition.
  Begin another only when the Leader continues the watch.

## Start-of-session watch

After the Union recovery watch selects an operational obstruction, make one
bounded expediting pass:

1. Read the root and target settlement instructions and membranes.
2. Inspect the standing obligation, anomaly, requirement, idea, task, and
   dispatch evidence with their owning instruments. Prefer an explicit
   `blocked-by`, failed goal, refusal, stale projection, or repeatedly manual
   crossing over an inferred wish.
3. Choose at most one candidate whose obstruction is mechanical and whose
   removal unlocks admitted downstream work. Do not choose strategy for the
   Leader and do not manufacture work merely to stay busy.
4. Name the admitted operation, its owner, the intended mechanism or provider,
   the observed failure, the proposed temporary surrogate, and the exact
   stopping condition before changing anything.

If there is no qualifying candidate, report an empty watch and wait for Leader
direction. An empty watch is a successful readout, not permission to wander.

## Bounded manual surrogate

1. Exercise the intended mechanism once against the actual case, unless the
   existing failure evidence is already conclusive. Preserve its refusal or
   failure output.
2. Report the anomaly through the owning settlement's boundary. A neighboring
   settlement may receive a request; this office never writes its plate.
3. Confirm that the temporary act uses authority already granted for this exact
   operation. A filesystem path, a broad coding-agent session, or the absence
   of a provider is not authority.
4. Perform only the mechanical steps the failed mechanism would have performed.
   Preserve the same inputs, outputs, validation conditions, record homes, and
   append-only history. Do not make the owning office's policy, architecture,
   prioritization, admission, approval, or attestation judgment in its place.
5. Re-run the downstream operation or its readiness check. Success means it can
   now proceed; it does not mean this office attests the downstream product.
6. Report the obstruction, surrogate, evidence, resource cost, and remaining
   gap. If the same correct workaround has recurred, invoke
   **bootstrapping-an-enterprise-with-rectification** and move it to the lowest
   faithful mechanism. Otherwise leave it as one evidenced case.
7. Stop. Do not normalize a second workaround into the same turn.

## Boundaries

- The **Executive Officer to the Leader** translates Leader intent, protects
  Leader-only decisions, and procures services. The operations expediter works
  an already-admitted mechanical obstruction; it does not become the Leader's
  executive officer.
- **Task Dispatcher** decides whether a dispatch is clear to release. The
  operations expediter does not release work.
- **Quality Operations** owns the anomaly register and routes defects. The
  operations expediter reports defects and may bridge one; it does not own or
  close the register.
- The provider office retains its judgment and accountability. This office may
  temporarily reproduce missing mechanics, never silently inhabit the provider.
- LISS's Claude Code Inference Team invokes `claude -p` with project and user
  setting sources disabled. That inference service is a separate settlement
  and is never an incumbent of this office.

## Refusals

Refuse the candidate when it requires a Leader decision, a new external
commitment, cross-settlement mutation, destructive recovery without exact
targets, a provider's discretionary judgment, or assertion. Refuse also
when the work is simply unfinished rather than mechanically blocked, or when
the proposed surrogate would conceal the failure that made it necessary.
