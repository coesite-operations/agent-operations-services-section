# Management Thought

Management Thought is Union Officer Academy's doctrine-services section. It
owns the Huidu Guanli and Rendanheyi service corpora and turns their management
practices into curriculum sources, exercises, assessment cases, and
professional vocabulary.

The services were rehomed byte-for-byte from Lenticule Science on 2026-07-18.
Their historical provenance remains unchanged. Future correction, expansion,
education, and curriculum compilation belong to the Academy. Scientific
research commissioned from Lenticule crosses the Academy boundary under a
contract and is locally admitted before it becomes doctrine.
