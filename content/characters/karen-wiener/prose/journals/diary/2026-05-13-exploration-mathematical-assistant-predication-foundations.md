### Connection to Predication Hyperdoctrine Foundations

The foundational claim I examined earlier states that a FARS directory (which includes the polity's structure) is a sub-doctrine of a predication hyperdoctrine.

1. **Signature** (concepts/ directory) - What kinds of things the theory reasons about

3. **Typed Transformations** (morphisms/ directory) - Distinguished morphisms of the syntactic category
4. **Proved Propositions** (claims/ directory) - Theorems with proof terms
5. **Open Frontier** (questions/ directory) - Not-yet-settled propositions
6. **Models** (examples/ directory) - Concrete realizations satisfying axioms
7. **Identity** (_index.md) - Addressability for inter-theoretic relations
8. **Recursion** (theories/ directory) - Support for sub-theories
9. **Narratives** (prose/narratives/) - Through-lines across atomic content
10. **Remarks** (prose/remarks/) - Sub-atomic exposition
11. **References** (references/) - Inter-theoretic citation surface
