## What I Learned

### 1. Governance Domain Applications

**Existing Predication Theory Applications in Governance:** - The `work/governance/persona/main/main.md` file explicitly frames the persona as a theory about itself (PyFARS - Python Flatfile Agentic Resource System) - Governance offices (architect, operations-officer, organizational-counsel) can be seen as operating within predication structures - The `_index.md` in `work/governance/agents/` references the flatfile-agentic-resource-system theory which examines how agent-based systems store resources as flat files - Governance questions and decision-making processes map naturally to predication settling as computation

**Potential Mappings:** - **SPO Triples in Governance**: - Subject: Governance office/role (e.g., architect) - Predicate: exercises/performs/oversees - Object: Governance function/domain (e.g., technical design, operations) - Value: Decision/output/state change from governance action - **Fibers in Governance**: Range of possible outputs/decisions for a given office-predicate combination - **Bilattice Values in Governance Epistemology**: - ⊥: No information/uncertainty about governance situation - t: Confirmed governance knowledge through evidence/process - f: Determined governance approach is false/refuted - ⊤: Conflicting governance principles or contradictory evidence - **Concept Lattices in Governance Skill Organization**: - Grouping governance capabilities by shared attributes (jurisdiction, authority type, etc.) - Hierarchy from general governance principles to specific office duties - **Closure in Governance Learning**: - Governance processes settling on stable decisions through evidence accumulation - Fixed points representing established governance precedents or policies - **Federation in Multi-Office Governance**: - Emergent governance capabilities that arise only from office interaction - Mayer-Vietoris sequence analyzing gains/losses in governance federation

### 2. Work Domain Applications

**Potential Mappings:** - **SPO Triples in Work**: - Subject: Worker/agent/team - Predicate: works on/requires/produces - Object: Project/task/skill/deliverable - Value: Work output/progress/state change - **Fibers in Work**: Range of possible work outputs for a given worker-project combination - **Bilattice Values in Work Epistemology**: - ⊥: No information about work status/progress - t: Confirmed work completion through verification - f: Determined work approach is ineffective/wrong - ⊤: Conflicting work requirements or contradictory progress indicators - **Concept Lattices in Work Skill Organization**: - Grouping work skills by shared attributes (tools used, domains, complexity) - Hierarchy from general work competencies to specific technical skills - **Closure in Work Mastery**: - Work settling on stable performance through practice and feedback - Fixed points representing mastered work competencies - **Federation in Collaborative Work**: - Emergent work capabilities from team collaboration - Analysis of what's gained/lost when individuals federate into teams
