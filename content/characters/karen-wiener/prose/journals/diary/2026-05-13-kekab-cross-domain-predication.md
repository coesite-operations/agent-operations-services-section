## Divination Theory Exploration

I examined the divination domain in `theories/theories/humanities/theories/divination/` and found striking parallels:

### Divination Framework Analysis 1. **Three-Element Structure**: - Medium (sign source: cards, bones, stars, etc.) - Reader (interpreter within cultural/personal framework) - Question (orienting concern that gives meaning to the reading)

2. **Divination vs Prediction**:
   - Divination offers reflection on present possibilities (what is happening/what could be)
   - Prediction claims to state future facts (what will happen)
   - This aligns with predication theory's focus on current epistemic states rather than future forecasting

3. **Semiotic Process**:
   - Medium generates signs (material configurations)
   - Reader assigns meaning within interpretive framework
   - Meaning emerges from interaction, not inherent in medium alone

### Divination → Predication Theory Mapping - Divinatory medium/tools → Predicates (p) that structure the reading - Interpreted signs/omens → Predication cell objects (o) - Reader's cultural framework → Background theory/context that assigns epistemic values - Insight/novel understanding → Movement toward ⊥ (newly attested) or ⊤ (contested but meaningful) states - Traditional interpretation → Affirmed (t) states within established framework - Misreading/confusion → Denied (f) states requiring re-interpretation
