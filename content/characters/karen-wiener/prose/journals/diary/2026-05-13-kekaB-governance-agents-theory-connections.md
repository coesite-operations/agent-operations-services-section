Precedent and Pattern Recognition Supports Subdirectory Approach Two existing subdirectories provide evidence for organizational patterns: - `emsemiote/` contains semiotic engineering agents, showing acceptance of subdirectory organization - `endpoints/` is intended for API/interface agents (currently empty) showing foresight about interface-focused agents
