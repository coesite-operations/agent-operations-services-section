# How to ground first-officer office functions in predication theory SPO triples?

## Resolution Based on predication theory principles from theoretical-overview.md and related documents, the first-officer office should be grounded as follows:

**Primary SPO Triple (Office Level):** - Subject: first-officer office (the entity being described) - Predicate: enacts/serves/performs (the core function) - Object: ship operational state including crew work, captain directives, and infrastructure - Value: Epistemic state of decisions (⊥=unknown, t=true/confirmed, f=false/refuted, ⊤=contradicted/contested)

**Skill-Level SPO Triples:** Each delegable skill forms its own SPO triple where: - Subject: first-officer office (consistent across skills) - Predicate: the specific skill being enacted (dispatch, interpret, design, etc.) - Object: what the skill acts on (work items, directives, architectural plans, etc.) - Value: Result/outcome of applying that skill (success/failure/partial success/etc.)

**Predication Fiber:** The first-officer office operates in the predication fiber defined by the fixed Subject-Predicate pair: - Subject: first-officer office - Predicate: enacts/serves/performs - Fiber: All possible values (outcomes) for different Objects (different situations, work types, directives) - This fiber forms a bilattice structure where office decisions can be compared and combined

**Bilattice Epistemology Handling:** The office should explicitly track its epistemic state when making decisions: - ⊥ (bottom): No information/unattested - when first encountering a novel situation - t (true): Confirmed/affirmed - when evidence supports a conclusion - f (false): Denied/refuted - when evidence contradicts a hypothesis - ⊤ (top): Contradicted/contested - when faced with conflicting evidence requiring judgment

**Concept lattice organization:** Skills should be organized by shared attributes: - Group by precondition types (what must be true before skill can apply) - Group by postcondition types (what the skill establishes) - Create hierarchy from general-purpose to specialized skills - Use Galois connection between skill capabilities and required contexts

**Closure conditions for settling:** The office has "settled" on a course of action when: - Evidence converges on stable conclusion (reaching fixed point in knowledge-order) - No new relevant information is expected that would change the decision - The decision has been validated against applicable constraints and duties
