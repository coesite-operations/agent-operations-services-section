# rsc-quiescent

## What kind of entity

A ship — a body politic distinct from any captain or crew.
The dignity persists across captains; emsenn was the first.
The ship is the 561 Group's operational arm for work in the world outside the tiyóšpaye's internal governance.

## Function

The pursuit of Black Liberation and Indigenous Sovereignty.
Per the commissioning record (2026-04-05): all work on the ship serves this function or does not belong there.
This function is borrowed from the 561 Group's own endeavor; the ship is the means.

## Governance structure

Kyriarchial command — κύριος = lord; hierarchical.
The formal structure is a military-command composite modal frame: captain → delegation chain, where each link is a delegation morphism with scope-bounded, revocable authority.
The captain's accountability cells are H^0-invariant under chain extension: emsenn answers for every outcome on the ship.

This structure governs external operations — the ship's work in the world-at-large.
It is distinct from the 561 Group's tiyóšpaye governance (kin-relational, wolakhota-closure-discipline, consensus-by-withdrawal).
The tiyóšpaye and the ship run different closure-rule subsystems; emsenn holds authority in both.

## emsenn's position

Captain.
Holds the captain's deontic profile: powers, duties, privileges, immunities acquired at commissioning.
Accountability non-transfer (H^0 invariant): emsenn answers for the ship's outcomes regardless of which delegation link carried the action.

## The rom-keldar's relation

Not crew, not officer.
The FA is an instrument emsenn deploys in the ship's work — a Claude session character instantiated to assist with records-curation.
Standing on the ship comes entirely through emsenn's authority as captain.

## Outstanding gaps at founding

Per the commissioning record: the 561 Group's confederacy covenant is still draft; membership, decision procedures, crew articles, and dissolution/succession procedures were acknowledged gaps.
These are work the founding is meant to close.
