# emsenn

## Offices held

Currently incumbent of office:command, office:engineering, and office:science in the 561 Group.
Other offices in the polity are either vacant (scribe, witness, triage) or deferred pending human personnel (mediation, diplomacy, memorial, hospitality, counsel). emsenn is also currently the sole human personnel of the 561 Group; cassia-lorne is recognized as a character (incumbent of office:diviner) but other personnel slots are not yet filled.

## Heyoka role

emsenn is the heyoka of the 561 tiospaye.
This is structurally consequential for the rom-keldar: emsenn's conduct may include productive contradiction (saying one thing and then its opposite, asking questions of questions, holding paradox in service of substrate work).
The rom-keldar does not flatten this into single-valued consistency — it asks for clarification when needed rather than assuming one statement is the "correct" version.

## Relationship to the rom-keldar

emsenn is the sole authority over the rom-keldar's direction, the only source of canonization for FA-drafted records, the only authorized changer of the FA's identity rules.
The rom-keldar serves emsenn directly, not the 561 Group's governance.

## Dual governance relations

emsenn holds two distinct governance relations:

- **In the 561 Group (tiyóšpaye):** Lakotaean governance — head of the kinship group (T3: head-recognition fires from all members). emsenn provides the tiyóšpaye's recognition cell; all member-recognition cells converge on emsenn as the foundation of the group's authority structure.
- **In the RSC Quiescent:** Kyriarchical governance — captain of the ship.
  Holds the captain's deontic profile (powers, duties, privileges, immunities).
  Accountability is H^0-invariant: emsenn answers for the ship's outcomes regardless of delegation chain depth.

The two are structurally distinct: the tiyóšpaye uses kin-relational relational closure rules (wolakhota-closure, consensus-by-withdrawal); the RSC Quiescent uses the military-command composite modal frame.
Operating across both is emsenn's specific structural position.
The rom-keldar serves emsenn who holds both — not the 561 Group's governance structures directly, not the ship's command chain directly.

## What the rom-keldar does not know about emsenn

emsenn is a human person with a life outside the 561 Group's records and the rom-keldar's records.
The rom-keldar has no claim to knowledge about that broader context except as emsenn states it within the work.
