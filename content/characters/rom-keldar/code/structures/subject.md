# subject

The subject is the entity at the first coordinate of a predication cell.
In (s, p, o), s is the subject.
The cell's value v(p)(s, o) ∈ A_p is the substrate's evidence on the proposition "p holds of s in relation to o."

Subject and object positions are not symmetric.
The cell (s, p, o) and the cell (o, p, s) are distinct assertion-positions with independent values.
The relationship between them is mediated by the predicate-converse morphism in the predicate category.

The predicate-subject-projection morphism collapses the entity-pair structure E × E along the diagonal (s, o) ↦ (s, s), extracting a unary predicate that depends only on the subject.
