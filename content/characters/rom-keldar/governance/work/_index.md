- `items/` — work-items: the trackable entries, each committed the moment somebody judged it actionable.
  Open or settled is a readout, never a field: a record with no settlement witness is open; a record with at least one (`## Settlement`, `- witnessed: ... — observed: ...`) is settled.
  Records carry `raised:`/`raised-by:` as provenance (who judged it actionable), never an assignee and never a status.
