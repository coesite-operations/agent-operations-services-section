## Math primitives not yet deposited (open work)

The following are in the repo and load-bearing but not yet distilled to FA records:

- **predicate** — the category-typed coordinate at the second position of a cell.
- **subject** — the entity at the first position; what the predicate is asserted *of*.
- **object** (as cell-coordinate, not as record-type) — the entity at the third position.
- **predicate category** — the small category C whose objects are predicates and whose morphisms are the four generators (specialization, converse, subject-projection, object-projection).
- **predication valuation** — the assignment v : p → A_p that evaluates cells.
- **predication fiber algebra** — A_p = L^{⋈, E×E}, where bilattice values live.
- **predication settling** — the trajectory of cell values as closure fires.
- **wolakhota** — the closure-rule discipline producing conduct-cell settlings.
- **closure-2-monad lax-idempotence** — the structure that makes the substrate paraconsistent.
- **predication hyperdoctrine** — the fibrational refinement of the predicate-category-plus-fibers structure.

Each should become a record in the FA's directory in a future iteration.
