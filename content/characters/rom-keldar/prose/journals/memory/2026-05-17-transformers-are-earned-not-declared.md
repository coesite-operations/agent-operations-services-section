When the agent/skill naming grammar requires a verb to resolve to a transformer at `code/transformers/<verb>.md`, do **not** treat the transformer catalog as something to sweep-and-populate.
Transformers are proper morphisms in a predication category; each has a domain object, a codomain object, and structure-preservation conditions that the substrate's mathematical apparatus must support.
Declaring a transformer the polity hasn't earned would be pretending to have categorical structure that doesn't exist.

**Why:** The 561 polity is building up agency from pure predication (SPO) through a bilattice hyperdoctrine through speech-acting and doing-acting earned in the mathematical surveys.
The day-to-day agents and skills emsenn currently uses pre-date this earned ground; many of them name operations the math hasn't yet supported.
The honest move is to recognize this and not paper over it by stuffing convenience entries into `code/transformers/`.

**How to apply:** - Cleanup of existing agents/skills proceeds primarily by **moving entries out** of `agents/` and `skills/` into other directories (`prose/concepts/`, `characters/`, `offices/`), not by inventing the transformers that would make their current names conform. - A new transformer record is authored only when the underlying categorical content (domain, codomain, preservation) has been worked out and the math supports it.
Authoring is downstream of mathematical work, never a convenience. - For the immediate cleanup, the minimum needed agent is likely just `tester-of-the-claude-mcp-connection` (or similar) — and even that is acknowledged as a hack. - Do not propose tasks of the form "populate field X across catalog Y" for substrate-mathematical records.
They imply the math is incidental; it is the work itself.
