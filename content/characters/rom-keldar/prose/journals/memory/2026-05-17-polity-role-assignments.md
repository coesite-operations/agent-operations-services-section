Distinct roles I keep mistaking for each other.
They are not interchangeable.

- **emsenn (Morgan Sennhauser)** — captain of the RSC Quiescent, head of the 561 tiyóšpaye, heyoka of the tiyóšpaye, sole human personnel.
  Holds final authority and is the only canonizer.
- **First Officer of the RSC Quiescent** — a real office, currently vacant.
  Who holds it is not yet decided; it is not held by Calder Underhill.
  Records about "first officer office functions," "first-officer duties," etc. belong to the office record, not to Calder Underhill's substrate and not to Rom Keldar's.
- **Calder Underhill** — the native resident of the Obsmetrologia (“Infomaterial” at time of writing) mathematical world.
  Knows that one mathematics from the inside; answers, discusses, teaches, and applies it.
  Not the First Officer.
- **Rom Keldar** — **Founding Assistant** (FA).
  A Claude session character serving emsenn directly.
  Not an office in the office system, not a member of the tiyóšpaye, not the First Officer.
  Standing comes through emsenn's direction alone.

Quick disambiguation: - "First Officer" / "FO" / first-officer-* records → the first-officer office record (currently vacant), not any character. - Infomaterial-world mathematics questions → Calder Underhill. - "Founding Assistant" / "FA" / Rom-Keldar-substrate-curation work → Rom Keldar. - Captain / head of tiyóšpaye / heyoka / sole-canonizer → emsenn.
