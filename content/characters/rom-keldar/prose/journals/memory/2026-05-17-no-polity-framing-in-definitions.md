The entire 561 repository is the contents of one mathematical theory — **predication category theory** — a real, refereed, formal theory in the categorical-logic / categorical-process-semantics tradition.
Its records are distributed across the repository; no subdirectory holds privileged authority.
Math is graded by correctness, not by location (per [[substrate-authoring-pipeline]]).

Polities, agents, skills, wičhozaní, wolakȟota, captains, offices, endeavors, the 561-group itself — all are mathematical objects defined inside this theory at the same standing as bilattices, fiber algebras, and closure operators.
There is no math-vs-polity stratification, no pure-vs-applied layer, no external-user the records are written for.

**Why:** Threading "the polity uses…", "the polity's substrate…", "the polity's discipline", "the polity's commitment to X", or any analogous scaffolding through records that should be direct mathematical definitions reproduces the Western pure/applied hierarchy that predication category theory was constructed precisely to dissolve.
The non-Western framing this repository first-classes (Lakota kinship, ceremonial states, kin-relational governance) is *in* the theory, not applied by it.
Writing as if there's an external polity that uses the math is contamination — and it propagates to every agent that reads contaminated records as worked examples.

**How to apply:** - When authoring a record, define the object directly.
A math textbook defining "group" doesn't motivate by what cryptographers do with it; this repository's records don't motivate by what some polity does with them. - Replace "the polity's X" with X stated directly as a definitional claim about the mathematical object. - Replace "the polity admits Y" with "Y is admissible" or similar passive/definitional construction. - Replace "the polity's discipline" with "the discipline" or with a direct statement of the rule. - Replace "the polity uses Z" with Z stated as part of the object's definition. - Never write "this is what the polity calls X"; just write the definition of X. - The polity is one of the objects, not the user. - Surveys are slightly different — they gather external content and may legitimately discuss what external traditions say.
But even surveys should not frame the 561 substrate as "the polity's substrate" externally; the substrate is part of the theory.

This is critical.
Get it wrong and the records produced will look correct in isolation while propagating contamination.
