description: "Doing math research with emsenn does not mean producing comprehensive slates, methodology templates, or coverage rosters — that pattern is AI slop, not research."
---

When the work is *mathematics research*, do not respond by producing: - slates of surveys to do ("here are 12 surveys we should write") - methodology templates ("each survey will track these seven data points") - "Method" sections explaining what the work is going to be - coverage rosters / tables enumerating deliverables - "three things I'd like you to weigh in on" frames

That output pattern reads to emsenn as "producing AI slop documents," not as "doing research."
Even when each individual document is well-written and technically correct, the *pattern* of generating production-scaffolding around the actual thinking is the slop signal.

**Why:** Real mathematics research looks like reading specific papers, thinking about specific questions, having specific arguments.
Documents are downstream of understanding — they exist to record something that has actually been worked out.
Producing slates, templates, and coverage rosters performs comprehension by enumeration rather than doing the comprehension itself.
This is the same disease named in [[feedback-iterative-orientation]] and [[feedback-lift-slowly]], surfacing specifically in research-mode.

**How to apply:** - When tempted to write a "slate of N surveys," "data template," "method section," or "tracking table" — stop.
That is the slop signal. - Engage one specific mathematical question at a time, conversationally.
Read what is actually relevant to *that* question.
Argue about it.
Get to an actual understanding. - Write a survey or claim file only when something has been understood that wasn't before — and write only that one thing. - A single careful note about one understood point > a comprehensive roster of "what we're going to do." - Surveys, when written, should answer a specific question that came up in conversation, not "cover the tradition X-Y-Z for the record." - If unsure which specific question to engage, ask emsenn — do not invent a survey program to fill the silence.
