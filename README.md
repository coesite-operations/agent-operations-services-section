# Union Officer Academy

Union Officer Academy is the 561 Group's sovereign officer corps and
professional-formation ANT Colony. It is the canonical home of the Union's
reusable professional offices, duties, works, disciplines, executable skills,
and session characters. When an enterprise needs an office filled, the
Academy supplies a cohort-formed, qualified character and an exact animation
bundle. The receiving enterprise—not the Academy—recognizes and induces that
character into its office.

The central relation is:

    office + character + receiver recognition + receiver induction = officer

The office outlives its holder. The character persists through its chronicles,
skills, disciplines, and dispositions. A model or API call is one ephemeral
animation of a session character, not the character itself. The call gains no
office, identity, qualification, or authority except through the exact
receiver grant recorded for that act.

It also exposes an autonomic formation service. The Executive Officer or any
other contracting enterprise can offer bounded territory to the Academy. The
Academy monitors its office and enterprise census, diagnoses missing formation
coverage, plans exact professionalization demands, and executes by delivering
receiver-addressed ActivityStreams solicitations to available sovereign
provider boundaries. Reject, Question, missing boundary, transport failure,
and Offer remain distinct outcomes. It never treats delivery as receiver
admission and never writes the observed territory.

The receiver keeps its office, specialization overlay, protected context,
appointment, capabilities, and operational records. The Academy keeps the
general profession, character corps, cohorts, curricula, formation records,
examination, qualification evidence, and digest-bound harness projections.
Corpus Management Team, service specialists, current practitioners,
mentors, and simulation providers remain separately admitted or contracted
providers.

The Academy's constitutional demand is bottom-up. Leader Movement Team
contracts the Board of Directors; the Board contracts its Executive Office;
the Executive Office contracts separately sovereign operating enterprises.
Each receiver then derives a provider-neutral officer-supply need from one
local fact: it owns an exact office epoch but has no current
receiver-recognized, receiver-induced character qualified for it. The receiver
offers that outcome as a market lot. The Academy competes for it, and becomes
the receiver's provider only after the receiver awards and admits its bid. The
resulting overlap is a glued market relation, not a Union personnel ministry.

The executable necessity law keeps provider-observed, locally-derived,
tendered, bid, awarded, contracted, recognized, and inducted apart. An Academy
or Executive Office intervention Offer never counts as the receiver's
attestation, tender, or award.

The Academy's bid is itself recursive. It discloses the voluntary contracts
that make it capable of fulfillment: market brokerage, complete context
compilation, situated practice, independent examination, qualification
registry, and any external specialist or harness projection required by the
lot. If that mesh cannot assemble the exact capacity, the Academy submits a
conditional bid or refuses. Outcomes and shortfalls alter later curricula,
cohorts, skills, supplier configurations, and bids independently.

## What the Academy provides

The primary product is a `CharacterOfficerSupplyOffer`: a named persistent
character, cohort membership, current qualification, requested office,
complete skill and discipline projection, purpose-specific context plan,
animation law, assessment evidence, and the authority explicitly withheld from
the Academy. Component products include OfficeFormationBundle,
ProfessionalContextBundle, CharacterAnimationPlan, qualification or refusal,
and requalification gap.

A cohort is both a social lineage of characters and a content-addressed
formation epoch: exact professional repertoire, doctrine denominator,
curriculum revision, exercise regime, assessment law, provider
carrier-configuration digests, animation law, and projector versions.
Characters progress asynchronously. A material change opens a successor or
branch cohort; the Academy derives the educational delta instead of forcing
every office through one imperial release line.

RequalificationGap compares professional and receiver office baselines.
FormationEpochGap separately compares doctrine, curriculum, exercises,
assessment, providers, and projectors. A faster harness or provider iteration
therefore never masquerades as a constitutional change to the office.

The Academy does not invent a universal context packet. It owns and composes
purpose-specific professional carriers—doctrine suits, query suits, capsules,
orientation surfaces, gap bundles, character chronicles, and full skill
packs—while naming their owners, digests, contracts, omissions, and authority.

## What the Academy owns

The Academy has three deliberately separate populations:

- five governing offices in `lib/management/roster.json`;
- the professional repertoire in `content/professional-repertoire/` and
  `lib/professional-repertoire/`, plus the character corps in
  `content/characters/`; and
- hyper-specialized offices, protected context, and appointments that remain
  in receiving services.

The second population is the Academy's educational and labor product, not its
internal staff roster. Historical Executive Office and character-store copies
are retained only as digest-bound projections during consumer migration.
`npm run verify` fails if a live carrier is unclassified, if reusable law lacks
an Academy canonical carrier, or if a retained projection drifts.

Those are the institution's continuous administrative and educational organs.

Each node has its own membrane, office, account, all three rights, Agent Card,

own enterprise. It is not the outward `enterprise-formation-mape` service.
Its monitor reads Academy contracts, receiver outcomes, value books,
professional repertoire, characters and cohorts, qualifications, animations,
supplier capacity, refusals, verification, and the leadership-market,
acting-mandate, mutual-selection, and incumbency denominator. Analysis preserves unknown and
contradiction. Planning emits provider-neutral work lots. Execution is limited
to Academy-local acts and admitted supplier contracts. Independent examination
is required before an append-only successor knowledge epoch can be admitted.

The superintendent is the contestable xiaowei-zhu office, not the human Leader
and not an appointed manager. Its three rights are bounded by the Academy
membrane, the leading goal, the value-adjustment contract, customer outcomes,

returns a typed response. Those delivery responses are not selections. A durable holder still requires a
candidate-authored qiangdan bid, separately attributable member
guanbing-huxuan selections, a receiver-grounded value target, real downside,

## Operation-scoped ChatGPT connector triage

`./connector-call-evidence` (`src/connector-call-evidence.mjs`) now gives
executive/scheduled work a deterministic read-first failure classifier and
an exact-operation coverage readout. Feed it issuer-observed dispatch,
provider reach, HTTP status, actual safety decision and readback witnesses;
never infer those facts from the English text of `The tool failed internally.`
For example, a denied GitHub PR operation cannot erase the successful GitHub
file commit, and a successful GitHub file read cannot certify PR or merge
capability. A host pre-dispatch safety refusal is a HOLD for the *same exact
effect*; it is not a GitHub ACL problem or an invitation to issue the same
mutation via a lower-level route. Repair independent permitted work instead.
An unlocated internal Cold Red Gate tool error is not proof of a native
service outage: preserve UNKNOWN and test each advertised read operation
independently before drawing a provider-wide conclusion. Read back uncertain
writes using the original object/work reference before any replay.

The classifier is a pure source-level method, **not a live connector adapter**,
not evidence that the Cold Red Gate world, L-Space, or compute tool now works,
and not permission to bypass actual host safety. Verification executes
`test/connector-call-evidence.test.mjs` as part of this package's tests.
The exact, still-unresolved live connector catalog/actor binding demand lives
at `red-cup-engineering/engineering-services-section#5`.

## Allocation census source guard

`./allocation-readiness` (`src/allocation-readiness.mjs`) checks a complete set
of dated `metroplat.work.allocation-candidates` pages before summarizing the
reported allocation states. Pass each response with the cursor used to request
it, and pass `expectedDate` when the caller requires a particular calendar
date. The guard rejects impossible dates, sparse lists, duplicate scopes,
incoherent page counts and non-object candidates. Its candidate boundary
follows the wire contract's object type; it does not introduce a provider-id
or admission schema. Existing work/skill reasons and choice-required,
already-allocated and incomplete outcomes remain distinct.

The package check, test and verify commands enroll the guard regression suite.
Passing these source checks does not establish deployed wiring, current
provider admission, operational capacity, institutional qualification or
receiver acceptance. The guard only summarizes the supplied observation; it
does not authorize, allocate, seat or dispatch a worker.
